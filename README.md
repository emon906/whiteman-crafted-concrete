# Whiteman Concrete Prestige

Create a premium, cinematic website for “Whiteman Concrete Construction LLC”, based in Fruitport, Michigan and serving West Michigan.

Business info:
• Quality residential flatwork
• Fully licensed & insured
• 10+ years of experience
• 100% recommended — 10 reviews
• Phone: +1 231-327-8861
• Email: codyjwhiteman2@gmail.com
• Location: Fruitport, MI 49415

DESIGN:
Create a bright luxury construction website, NOT a generic contractor template.
Use a premium blue + white + light concrete-gray palette with subtle sky-blue accents.
Absolutely NO dark theme, NO chocolate/brown, NO black-heavy sections.

HERO:
Create a unique cinematic hero with a concrete pouring/finishing video.
Use oversized typography, an offset project image, floating glass cards and a premium badge.
Headline: “Built to Last. Finished With Pride.”
CTA: “Request a Free Quote”
Secondary CTA: “View Our Work”

SECTIONS:
1. Minimal premium navbar
2. Cinematic video hero
3. Trust stats: 10+ Years / Licensed / Insured / 100% Recommended
4. Residential concrete services with large project images
5. Full-width VIDEO-ONLY concrete work section
6. “A Decade of Quality” story section with image + text
7. Before/After project gallery
8. Premium masonry layout mixing images and short videos
9. Featured projects section
10. Full-width construction video with floating CTA
11. Why Choose Whiteman Concrete
12. Customer reviews
13. Large “Start Your Project” CTA
14. Premium footer

Make every section visually different. Mix full-width videos, image cards, asymmetric layouts, large typography and clean white space so the website never feels boring.

Use relevant concrete construction images and short concrete-work videos as placeholders.
Videos: autoplay, muted, loop, lazy-load.

Add smooth scrolling, subtle reveal animations, light parallax, elegant hover effects and polished transitions.
Keep everything lightweight and fast.

Make the website fully responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop.

IMPORTANT:
The final result must feel like an expensive $10,000+ construction company website — clean, bright, architectural, cinematic and highly professional.
Do NOT make it look like a normal concrete contractor website.
Do NOT use dark sections or brown/chocolate colors.
